# Changelog

## 0.1.0rc1 — 2026-09-18

Initial developer handover implementing specification v2.0: app-owned KPI schema, immutable submitted agreements, full-period review generation, employee-only self assessment, scoped final review, review-specific applicability, exact normalized scoring, correction/audit controls, scoped reports, notifications and scorecards.

Targets Frappe/ERPNext/HRMS v15 with MariaDB. Staging integration, concurrency and business UAT are required before production acceptance. See `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.
